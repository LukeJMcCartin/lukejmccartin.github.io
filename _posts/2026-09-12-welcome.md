Welcome to my website! 

This site is a GitHub pages site that serves as a growing resource for coding tutorials that I have developed. Thus far, these include pipelines for the analysis of metabarcoding data generated using both Illumina short read and Oxford Nanopore long read data.

**A bit about me** 

I earned my PhD from Lehigh University in the Department of Biological Sciences and the [Lehigh Oceans Research Center](https://www.lehighoceans.org/). Currently, I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History working on the [Mesophotic and Deep Benthic Communities Partnership](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/research/invertebrate-zoology/research/MDBC) between the museum and NOAA.
