Welcome to my website! 

I am Luke McCartin, PhD. I earned my PhD from Lehigh University in the Department of Biological Sciences and the [Lehigh Oceans Research Center](https://www.lehighoceans.org/).

Currently, I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History working on the [Mesophotic and Deep Benthic Communities Partnership](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/research/invertebrate-zoology/research/MDBC) between the museum and NOAA.

Please feel free to browse this site to learn more about my research, teaching, and outreach vision and experiences.
