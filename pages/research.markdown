My research broadly seeks to understand how the biodiversity and composition of species assemblages vary spatiotemporally on contemporary timescales, and how that knowledge can inform ecosystem conservation and restoration. A comprehensive understanding of where and when species and assemblages occur is vital to predict how they will respond to environmental change during our current biodiversity crisis. My work fills these gaps in knowledge by actively developing ‘omics tools, including environmental DNA (eDNA) sequencing, and pairing these new methods with conventional ecological and taxonomic approaches and principles.
